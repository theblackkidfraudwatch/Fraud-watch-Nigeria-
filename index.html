# 🛡️ Fraud Watch Nigeria **Exposing Scammers. Protecting Nigerians.** --- ## What Is This? Fraud Watch Nigeria is a website built to help Nigerians report scammers and avoid being scammed. Too many Nigerians lose money every day to fake investments, romance scams, fake vendors, and crypto scams. This project is here to stop that. ## Common Scams We Fight - 📱 Fake Investment / Ponzi Schemes - 💕 Romance Scams - 🏦 Fake Bank Alerts - 🎓 Fake JAMB / Admission Scams - 🛒 Fake Online Vendors - 💻 Job & Crypto Scams ## How To Report A Scam 1. Take screenshot of the scammer's number / chat / account 2. Send it to us on TikTok: @theblackkidfraudwatch 3. We will verify and expose them to warn others ## Built With - HTML - CSS ## Founder **TheBlackKid** - Criminology Student, Abuja, Nigeria Founder of Fraud Watch Nigeria ## Live Website 👉 https://theblackkidfraudwatch.github.io/fraud-watch-nigeria/ --- ⭐ If you believe in a scam-free Nigeria, star this repo and follow me.
